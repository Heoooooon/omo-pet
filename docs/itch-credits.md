# itch.io page text: characters and credits

Paste into the itch.io kit (page description, "Credits" block) and the
download's readme. Keep the Jabdori line wherever the app is listed.

## English

**Free:** Omo the kitten and Jabdori the puppy, with every move (walking on
windows, rocket, jet, parachute, multi-monitor, friends). Both come out on
first launch; hide either one in Settings > Characters.

**Band pack (paid):** Dalli, Bara, Dochi and Rupa, plus band mode.

낙하산, 여러 모니터, 친구 부르기). 처음 켜면 둘 다 나오고, 설정 > 캐릭터에서
하나씩 숨길 수 있어요.
