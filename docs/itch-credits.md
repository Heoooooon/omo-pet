# itch.io page text: characters and credits

Paste into the itch.io kit (page description, "Credits" block) and the
download's readme. Keep the OmO and Jabdori lines wherever the app is listed.

## English

**Free:** OmO, Jabdori the puppy and Omo the kitten, with every move (walking on
windows, rocket, jet, parachute, multi-monitor, friends). OmO and Jabdori come
out on first launch; show or hide each one, Omo included, in Settings > Characters.

**Band pack (paid):** Dalli, Bara, Dochi and Rupa, plus band mode.
