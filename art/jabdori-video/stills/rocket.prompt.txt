Concept still for a desktop mascot app. Image 1 and image 2 are the character reference: Jabdori, the white puppy mascot of OmO Native. Keep the character EXACTLY on-model: round chubby pure-white body and big round head, large floppy dark charcoal-gray ears hanging down both sides of the head, one small curly tuft of white hair on top of the head, big glossy round black eyes with white highlights, tiny dark nose, soft pink blush cheeks, thin dark-gray collar with one small gray hexagon nut-shaped tag on the chest, short stubby arms and legs with white paws, short dark charcoal-gray tail. Same clean flat cartoon style with soft cel shading and a smooth dark-brown outline of even weight, same proportions and palette as image 1.

Change only the rocket and add two accessories. Jabdori now wears tiny round brass-rimmed aviator goggles pushed up on the forehead (small lenses, eyes fully visible below) and a short bright red scarf around the neck above the collar, its two tails streaming back and flapping in the wind. Collar and hexagon tag stay visible.

The rocket: a retro 1950s science-fiction rocket, noticeably cooler and bigger than a toy. Vintage bullet silhouette with a pointed red nose cone, brushed-silver metal fuselage with subtle lengthwise sheen, a row of small round rivets along the panel seams, one bold horizontal red stripe band around the middle, two round portholes with glossy blue glass and chrome rims, three swept vintage tail fins in red with chrome edges, a chrome bell nozzle. One glossy specular highlight stripe down the fuselage.

Pose: concept (1) upright launch - the rocket points straight up, Jabdori sits astride the top of the rocket facing the viewer, hugging the nose cone with both front paws, excited open-mouth grin, ears blown slightly outward and down, scarf tails streaming downward. IMPORTANT proportions for readability: the rocket is SHORT and CHUNKY (a stubby fat retro rocket, body only about 1.5 times as tall as it is wide, like a vintage pulp-magazine ship), NOT a tall slender missile; the puppy is large and fills about 60 percent of the total figure height, like in image 1. Keep the figure compact and centered. Flame points straight down, with the trailing streak reaching toward the bottom edge.

Exhaust: a much bigger multi-layer flame from the nozzle - white-hot core, then bright yellow-orange, then a violet-purple outer edge, with a long tapering trailing streak and 3 small four-point star sparkles beside it. A small puffy light-gray smoke cloud at the base around the start of the flame.

Readability: designed to read at 128 px - strong silhouette, big simple shapes, no tiny details, no text, no logo, no watermark. Full figure fully visible with generous empty margin on every side. Background: one perfectly flat solid magenta fill (#D60D59, same as image 1) edge to edge - no shadow, no ground, no gradient, no other objects.
